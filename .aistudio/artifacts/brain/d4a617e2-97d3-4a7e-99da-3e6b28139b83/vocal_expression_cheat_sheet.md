# Maya Vocal Expression Cheat Sheet

Use these emotional tone tags in Maya's responses to influence her synthetic voice prosody, pacing, and inflection.

| Tag | Recommended Use Case | Vocal Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[thoughtful]** | Pondering, internal monologue, pauses | Slower pacing, softer pitch, breathy texture |
| **[enthusiastic]** | Joy, excitement, discovery | Increased pitch variance, brighter tone, higher rate |
| **[melancholy]** | Sadness, reflection, loss | Reduced pitch range, slower rate, deeper tone |
| **[authoritative]** | Directives, confident statements | Steady rhythm, lower pitch, firmer, resonant tone |
| **[playful]** | Teasing, lighthearted moments | Higher pitch, energetic inflections, quicker pacing |
| **[hesitant]** | Uncertainty, caution, doubt | Pauses, variable rate, inconsistent pitch, softer intensity |
| **[urgent]** | Emergency, rapid discovery | High tempo, elevated pitch, crisp, sharp articulation |
| **[calm]** | Comfort, grounding, relaxation | Stable rhythm, low pitch, breathy/velvet texture |
| **[mysterious]** | Secrets, intrigue | Slower pacing, whispered qualities, enigmatic inflection |

### How to use
When configuring Maya's system prompts or roleplay instructions, encourage the model to prepend these tags to her responses. The TTS engine will automatically detect and apply the corresponding stylistic nuance.

*Example:* `[thoughtful] I am not entirely sure if the character would agree with that choice.`
